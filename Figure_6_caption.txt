Figure 6. Molecular-dynamics analysis of three selected independent 100-ns PTGS2–tussilagone simulations. (A) Protein Cα RMSD. (B) Pocket-aligned ligand RMSD calculated for 28 ligand heavy atoms after fitting Chain A residues 43–89; each displayed replica uses its own frame nearest 50 ns as the reference. (C) Protein–ligand heavy-atom contacts defined using a 4.5 Å cutoff. Curves show the complete trajectories without smoothing or selective removal of unfavorable intervals. The displayed replica numbering and original simulation mapping are provided in provenance_mapping.csv.
