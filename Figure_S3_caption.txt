Supplementary Figure S3. Additional convergence and interaction analysis of three selected independent 100-ns PTGS2–tussilagone simulations. (A) Protein Cα RMSF. (B) Protein radius of gyration. (C) Replica-specific residue contact occupancy, calculated as the number of valid frames containing at least one heavy-atom contact within 4.5 Å divided by the total number of valid frames. (D) Joint post-50-ns ligand-pose clustering in a common pocket-fitted coordinate system using k-means with k=3. (E) Distribution of pocket-aligned ligand RMSD values during the final 50 ns. (F) Cross-replicate representative-pose RMSD matrix calculated using the common pocket-fitting definition and the 28-atom ligand mapping. These analyses distinguish persistent contacts, within-trajectory conformational stability, and cross-replicate pose convergence; they do not establish binding affinity.
